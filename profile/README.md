## Meet netplex

netplex is a network lab built by network engineers who were tired of fighting their tools. You draw a topology, press play, and real vendor images boot as genuine virtual machines and containers - not simulations. Consoles open in your browser, links show what is really crossing them, and nothing needs installing on your laptop.

It runs on your own server, and it keeps working with no internet at all.

## How it works

1. **Install** netplex on a server or virtual machine.
2. **Drop in your images** - copy them into the uploads folder and netplex recognises them and gets them ready, no permission fixing needed.
3. **Draw and play** - drag devices onto the canvas, connect them, power them on and start labbing.
